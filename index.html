01 — The distance

Different places.
One little world.

You have your streets, your skies, and your days in Bucharest. I have mine here in Kolkata. There are kilometres between us, and sometimes the clock seems determined to remind us of it.

But I like that our worlds found a way to overlap. Somewhere between ordinary conversations, silly jokes, and the little things you tell me, you became someone I look forward to.

“If I could fold the distance between us, I would still take the long way — just to have more time on the way to you.”

03 — A little poem for you

Two skies, two cities,
and one thought that travels far.

It crosses all the miles
without a map or car.

And if the night feels endless,
look up and you might see —

04 — And you, Petra

There is no grand formula.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, or that distance will always feel easy. I can promise that I want to keep showing up, keep listening, keep learning you, and keep choosing kindness when the miles get annoying.

I don't want you to feel like you have to be anything other than yourself around me. Your happy days, your stressful days, your quiet days — there's room for all of them.

✦

One thing I hope you know

You are not just a notification on my screen. You're a person I care about, with a whole universe of your own — and I feel lucky that you let me see little pieces of it.
